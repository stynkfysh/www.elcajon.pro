North County Inland

14 San Diego County communities, 10 with in-person service and 4 desktop only.

Inland North County is valley and hill country, warmer than the coast and once covered in groves. Escondido, Vista and San Marcos grew from farm towns into cities, with older homes near their downtowns and newer tracts on the surrounding slopes. San Elijo Hills is a planned community with association dues and special taxes. Valley Center, Fallbrook, Bonsall, Pauma Valley and Rainbow hold larger acreage, avocado and citrus groves, private wells, septic systems and some horse properties. Harmony Grove, Del Dios and Hidden Meadows sit in rugged terrain shaped by wildfire. Groves, water costs, access roads and fire exposure all affect value.

Site visits have a boundary. The two inspected formats, Drive-By and Standard (single-family or 2-4 unit), are sold only for addresses in the 68 places on Brian's visit list. That list holds 33 San Diego County names, El Cajon first among them for this site, plus 35 in Riverside County. For any other California address, the San Diego County backcountry included, the order becomes a desktop report. Check the visit list.
