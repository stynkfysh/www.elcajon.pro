East County Valleys and Hills

18 San Diego County communities, 15 with in-person service and 3 desktop only.

East of San Diego the land rises in waves of granite hills and oak-lined canyons, and the communities around El Cajon reflect that terrain. La Mesa and Lemon Grove are older streetcar and orchard towns with prewar cottages beside postwar tracts. Santee and Lakeside spread along the San Diego River, with newer subdivisions and horse properties. Mount Helix, Crest, Harbison Canyon and Alpine climb into steeper ground where custom homes, private wells, septic systems and wildfire hazard zones come into play. Spring Valley, Casa de Oro and Rancho San Diego fill the land toward Sweetwater. Values swing with elevation, lot size and the view, so comparables must stay on the same kind of ground.

Site visits have a boundary. The two inspected formats, Drive-By and Standard (single-family or 2-4 unit), are sold only for addresses in the 68 places on Brian's visit list. That list holds 33 San Diego County names, El Cajon first among them for this site, plus 35 in Riverside County. For any other California address, the San Diego County backcountry included, the order becomes a desktop report. Check the visit list.
