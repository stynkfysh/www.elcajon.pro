San Diego County area

Coast, Bays and Mesas

18 San Diego County communities, 18 with in-person service and 0 desktop only.

From La Jolla south to Point Loma, the city of San Diego meets the Pacific along bluffs, beaches and bays, and the mesas behind them carry decades of tract building. Beach cottages in Ocean Beach and Mission Beach sit on small lots near the sand, with coastal zone permits shaping what can be rebuilt. La Jolla and Point Loma hold custom homes where a view can account for much of the value. Inland, Clairemont, Bay Park, Serra Mesa and Allied Gardens are postwar neighborhoods with many remodeled ranch homes, and Tierrasanta and Carmel Valley are planned communities. View corridors, canyon frontage, airport or freeway noise and coastal rules all affect value.

Site visits have a boundary. The two inspected formats, Drive-By and Standard (single-family or 2-4 unit), are sold only for addresses in the 68 places on Brian's visit list. That list holds 33 San Diego County names, El Cajon first among them for this site, plus 35 in Riverside County. For any other California address, the San Diego County backcountry included, the order becomes a desktop report. Check the visit list.
