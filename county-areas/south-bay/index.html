South Bay and the Border

10 San Diego County communities, 9 with in-person service and 1 desktop only.

South of downtown, the bay curves toward the international border and the land climbs from tidal flats to mesas. National City and the older parts of Chula Vista hold early twentieth century homes and postwar tracts on a regular street grid. East of Interstate 805, Eastlake and Otay Ranch are master-planned communities built mostly after 1990, many with homeowners associations and Mello-Roos assessments that affect value. Bonita keeps larger lots and horse properties along the Sweetwater River. Imperial Beach and Coronado sit on the Silver Strand with ocean exposure, while San Ysidro, Nestor and Otay Mesa border the crossing. Special taxes, association dues and flood mapping all need attention here.

Site visits have a boundary. The two inspected formats, Drive-By and Standard (single-family or 2-4 unit), are sold only for addresses in the 68 places on Brian's visit list. That list holds 33 San Diego County names, El Cajon first among them for this site, plus 35 in Riverside County. For any other California address, the San Diego County backcountry included, the order becomes a desktop report. Check the visit list.
