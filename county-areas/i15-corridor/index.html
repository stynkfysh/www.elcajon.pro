Interstate 15 Corridor

12 San Diego County communities, 12 with in-person service and 0 desktop only.

Interstate 15 cuts north through the county's interior mesas, and most of the neighborhoods along it were planned between the 1970s and the 2010s. Mira Mesa, Rancho Peñasquitos and Scripps Ranch hold tract homes on curving streets, with eucalyptus groves in Scripps Ranch and canyon preserves throughout. Rancho Bernardo grew around golf courses and has many single-level homes and age-restricted areas. Carmel Mountain Ranch, Sabre Springs, 4S Ranch, Del Sur and Black Mountain Ranch are newer, often with associations and Mello-Roos. Poway keeps larger lots and horse zoning. Wildfire history, special taxes and association rules weigh heavily in this corridor's values.

Site visits have a boundary. The two inspected formats, Drive-By and Standard (single-family or 2-4 unit), are sold only for addresses in the 68 places on Brian's visit list.
