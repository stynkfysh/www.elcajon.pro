North County Coast

10 San Diego County communities, 10 with in-person service and 0 desktop only.

North of Torrey Pines, coastal cities run up the shore to Camp Pendleton, each with its own lagoon, bluffs and older beach towns. Del Mar and Solana Beach are small and built on bluff and hillside lots with ocean views. Encinitas spreads across Leucadia, Cardiff-by-the-Sea and Olivenhain, which keeps an equestrian, rural character. Carlsbad combines an older village with large master-planned communities, and Oceanside holds older beach neighborhoods along with inland tracts. Rancho Santa Fe and Fairbanks Ranch sit back from the coast with estate lots and covenants. Bluff erosion, coastal permits, view rights and lot size all count heavily here.

Site visits have a boundary. The two inspected formats, Drive-By and Standard (single-family or 2-4 unit), are sold only for addresses in the 68 places on Brian's visit list. That list holds 33 San Diego County names, El Cajon first among them for this site, plus 35 in Riverside County. For any other California address, the San Diego County backcountry included, the order becomes a desktop report. Check the visit list.
