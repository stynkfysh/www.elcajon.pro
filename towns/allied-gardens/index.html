About Allied Gardens

Allied Gardens occupies a mesa north of Interstate 8, with the San Diego River and Mission Gorge Road running along its western and northern flank. Waring Road and Zion Avenue form the local spine, and Navajo Road leads east toward San Carlos and Lake Murray. The City groups it with Grantville in one community plan, and the Green Line trolley stop at Grantville sits just below the mesa. Mission Trails Regional Park begins a short drive up the gorge, framing the community with chaparral ridges.

Housing you find here

Builders put up most of the homes in a rush during the mid-1950s, producing long blocks of single-story ranch houses with attached garages on lots often near 6,000 square feet. Layouts usually range from roughly 1,100 to 1,600 square feet, and three bedrooms is typical. Many owners have opened up kitchens, added a primary suite or built a detached ADU. Western rim lots look across the river gorge, and newer apartments and townhomes have gone up in the Grantville area along Mission Gorge Road.

Value levers in Allied Gardens

Buyers pay most for updated interiors, usable flat yards and lots with canyon outlooks. Easy reach to Mission Trails, Lake Murray and the trolley helps demand, while homes facing Waring Road or Zion Avenue absorb traffic noise. Original 1950s systems, including older electrical panels and galvanized plumbing, push condition adjustments up. Ongoing multifamily growth in Grantville adds services and transit traffic, and buyers weigh both sides when choosing blocks nearby.

Notes for an appraisal here

Tract repetition makes the sales comparison approach dependable, provided condition is rated consistently across comparables. Permit status of added bedrooms, converted garages and ADUs should be confirmed with the City. Lots on the gorge rim may sit in brush management zones and carry slope limitations. Retrospective dates sometimes require sales from before Grantville's newer apartments opened, since market tone on the lower streets has shifted over time.

Will an original 1955 kitchen hurt the trust value of an Allied Gardens home?

An original kitchen moves the home into a different comparable group rather than producing a penalty on its own. Brian looks for Allied Gardens and Del Cerro sales with similar dated interiors, then adjusts for size, lot and outlook. A buyer planning a remodel sets the price for homes like this, and the report explains that reasoning plainly for the trustee and beneficiaries.
